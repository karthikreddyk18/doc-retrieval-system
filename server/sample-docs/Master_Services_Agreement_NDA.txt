MASTER SERVICES & MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)
Contract Reference: MSA-2025-ALPHA-098
Effective Date: November 1, 2025

PARTIES:
1. Apex AI Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Disclosing Party" / "Provider")
2. Global Enterprise Solutions LLC, a California corporation ("Receiving Party" / "Client")

RECITALS:
The parties wish to explore a collaborative evaluation of Provider's Agentic AI Document Discovery and Intelligent Search Platform across Client's corporate Google Drive repositories and local engineering network file stores.

TERMS & CONDITIONS:

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" refers to all proprietary software, API integration tokens, algorithmic indexing methodologies, customer telemetry, financial statements, and technical roadmaps disclosed either orally or in writing.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY
The Receiving Party agrees to:
a. Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, exercising at least the same degree of care it utilizes for its own sensitive data (and never less than reasonable care).
b. Refrain from reverse engineering, disassembling, or decompiling any software binaries or AI function calling schemas.
c. Limit disclosure solely to employees, contractors, and legal advisors who need access to perform the mutual evaluation and who are bound by non-disclosure terms no less protective than this Agreement.

3. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY ASSURANCES
Provider warrants that document indexing routines are ephemeral: parsed document contents retrieved from Client's Google Drive or local storage are processed in transient memory and not permanently stored without Client's affirmative written opt-in.

4. TERM & TERMINATION
This Agreement remains in effect for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate with thirty (30) days prior written notice.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the authorized representatives of the parties have executed this Agreement.
