# Global Employee Handbook & Hybrid Work Guidelines
Effective: Fiscal Year 2025/2026
Applies To: All Full-Time Employees, Contractors, and Interns

## 1. Remote Work Culture & Core Hours
We believe in asynchronous autonomy paired with focused collaboration. Team members are expected to be available for synchronous team standups and client engagements during core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM in their respective local time zones.

## 2. Technology & Equipment Stipend
- **Home Office Setup**: All new team members receive a one-time $1,500 reimbursement for ergonomic desk chairs, external 4K monitors, and noise-cancelling headphones.
- **Monthly Connectivity**: A monthly $100 stipend is provided automatically on payroll to offset home broadband and cellular data costs.
- **Hardware Refresh**: Engineering laptops (MacBook Pro M3/M4 or ThinkPad Linux workstations) are refreshed every 24 months.

## 3. Information Security & Document Management
- **Device Encryption**: FileVault (macOS) or BitLocker (Windows) must remain enabled at all times.
- **Cloud Storage**: Corporate documentation must be stored either in designated Google Drive Workspace folders or committed to corporate Git repositories. Never store sensitive credentials in plaintext files on unencrypted local drives.
- **Document Classification**: Documents containing financial records, client NDAs, or security audit reports must be tagged as "Confidential - Internal Only".

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Wellness
- Flexible Paid Time Off: We observe 25 days of recommended vacation annually in addition to 11 recognized public holidays.
- Mental Wellness Days: One Friday each quarter is designated a global company recharge day with no internal meetings allowed.
